\begin{frame}{Constant Marginal Tax Rate}
    \mycomment{
    To avoid the welfare trap, a policy should include a constant (or say: continuos) marginal tax rate. There are fundamentally two equivalent ways to achive this: 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item universal basic income
        \item negative taxes
    \end{itemize}
    }
